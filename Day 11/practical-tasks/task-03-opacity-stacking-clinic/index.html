Stack A — opacity group

Stack B — rgba overlay

  • opacity: 0.5 on a parent and opacity: 0.5 on a nested child produce an effective opacity of 0.5 × 0.5 = 0.25 for the child's rendered content. Alpha transparency on a background does not reduce the opacity of its text.
