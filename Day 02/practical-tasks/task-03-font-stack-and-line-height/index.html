Typography lab

In this lab, you will practice using a font stack and line-height to improve the readability of body text on laptops.

Readable line-length is crucial for enhancing the user experience on laptops, as it directly impacts how easily users can process and comprehend text. When lines of text are too long, readers may struggle to maintain focus and lose their place while reading. Conversely, lines that are too short can disrupt the flow of reading and make the content feel disjointed. By optimizing line-length through appropriate font stacks and line-height settings, designers can create a more comfortable and engaging reading environment, ultimately improving comprehension and retention of information.
