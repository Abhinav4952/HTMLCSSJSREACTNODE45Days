Subsection: Importance of Headings

Headings play a crucial role in guiding readers through content. They break down information into manageable sections, making it easier to scan and comprehend. Proper use of heading levels (H1, H2, H3) ensures a logical flow of information.

Additionally, headings improve accessibility for screen readers, allowing visually impaired users to navigate content effectively. By following best practices in heading usage, developers can create inclusive web experiences.
